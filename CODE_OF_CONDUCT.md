# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free community for contributors and users, regardless of background, identity, experience, or location.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-based.
- Critique technical work without attacking people.
- Protect user security, privacy, and confidential disclosures.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Respect project licenses, contributor rights, and upstream communities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, deceptive claims, plagiarism, security exploitation, or disclosure of private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens the community or project. Reports should be sent privately to `contact@aifinpay.company`; use the subject `SECURITY: Stellar x402 Facilitator` when they include security or sensitive personal information.

Good-faith reports will be handled confidentially to the extent possible. Retaliation is prohibited.
