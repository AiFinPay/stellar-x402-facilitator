# SCF Build submission criteria checklist

Use this list for the final portal review. It summarizes the official criteria; the official handbook and rules remain authoritative.

## Completeness and prescreen

- [ ] Submission is understandable without reviewers opening external links.
- [ ] Problem, users, solution, Stellar relevance, architecture, deliverables, milestones, budget and team are included.
- [ ] Deliverables are concrete and testable rather than feature labels.
- [ ] Architecture exists before submission and covers trust boundaries, state and integrations.
- [ ] Amount is calculated from future eligible work and is not inflated.
- [ ] No marketing, advertising, past-work reimbursement, legal/entity or token costs are requested.
- [ ] Product status and traction are stated accurately; planned features are not described as shipped.

## Eligibility

- [ ] Applicant organization and every named contributor satisfy official eligibility rules.
- [ ] Geographic and sanctions restrictions have been checked for all contributors.
- [ ] KYC for contributors or KYB for the organization is feasible.
- [ ] At least two eligible representatives can attend required program sessions and demo activities.
- [ ] Project is lawful and does not involve prohibited gambling, fraud, sanctions evasion or similar activity.

## Technical merit

- [ ] Stellar usage is necessary and clearly explained.
- [ ] Implementation uses canonical `@x402/stellar` and respects the RFP constraints.
- [ ] Team skills and availability match the delivery plan.
- [ ] Security, privacy, testing, operations and maintenance are addressed.
- [ ] Open-source license and public development plan are explicit.
- [ ] Differentiation and benefit to other Stellar teams are evidenced.

## Award structure

- [ ] Four payments and acceptance gates are reflected: 10% award, 20% MVP, 30% testnet, 40% mainnet.
- [ ] Each funded tranche can be completed within the applicable 90-day window.
- [ ] Evidence exists for each acceptance criterion.
- [ ] Dependencies outside the team's control, including upstream approval, are not promised as guaranteed outcomes.

## Final integrity check

- [ ] Every team profile, metric, transaction hash, customer, partnership and production claim is verified.
- [ ] All placeholders are removed.
- [ ] Links work without login.
- [ ] Repository is public.
- [ ] License headers and third-party notices are current.
- [ ] Final budget total matches the portal amount and milestone percentages.
