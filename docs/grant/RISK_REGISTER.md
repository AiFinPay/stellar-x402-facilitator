# Delivery risk register

Risks are owned by named delivery leads and tied to observable triggers. A triggered high/critical risk is reviewed in the weekly delivery checkpoint and, where it can affect an SCF acceptance gate, recorded with the corresponding mitigation evidence.

## Escalation policy

- **Critical fund-safety risks** block the affected release until remediated or formally dispositioned through the applicable SCF/Audit Bank process.
- **High risks** that can invalidate a milestone are linked in the evidence register with mitigation and retest results.
- Scope, schedule or budget changes caused by a risk must document the impact before resources are reallocated.
